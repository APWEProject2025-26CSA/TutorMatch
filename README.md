# AP WE Project 2025-26 for AP CSA

##Introduction

TutorMatch will improve the education and learning of students across the globe and provide them with extra support. It will match students with peer tutors so they will do well with tutors who will best support their learning using our application. 

##Problem & Purpose

Education should be equitable and affordable, and that is our goal. Some students may not have access to the same resources as others and we want to bridge that gap. With TutorMatch, which is accessible, effective, and completely free, this project aims to provide equal and engaging education for all. 

##The Solution

TutorMatch utilizes a unique approach to give each of our students a more personalized and valuable academic experience. Using enhanced matching techniques through academic and behavioral analysis, it will give the best support needed to each student. A peer tutoring design allows for a more collaborative and engaging learning environment. TutorMatch is free to use and tracks student performance over time.

##Implementation

TutorMatch uses several Java classes to keep track of users and their academic "ratings" and behaviors. Each tutor receives ratings from their students and each student is rated by the tutor, which helps to effectively match tutors and students appropriately to their ability levels. An algorithm was developed in order to match tutors with the appropriate students.
