# AP With WE Project 2025-26 for AP CSA - TutorMatch

## Introduction:
In our school, we find that there are numerous students requiring extra academic support during school hours, and are unable to access them due to funding cuts for our afterschool club programs such as tutoring help with teachers, and now only certain students are able to get in touch with teachers to receive extra help during certain periods of the day, lacking a formal process to seamlessly connect with them and other well-versed students. TutorMatch will improve the education and learning of students across the globe and provide them with extra support by integrating into a 5 block school schedule,  and providing a simple but powerful platform for them to access the help they need to succeed. This tool will freely match students with teachers and peer tutors so they can learn best from knowledgeable members of the community, using our application.

## Problem & Purpose:

TutorMatch aims to address the issue of inaccessible education to those who are having difficulty with academics. Many students do not have access to the extra resources that they may require to succeed in their courses. TutorMatch intends to solve this issue by providing an accessible, easy to use means to match students with tutors who can provide valuable assistance. Tutors will be able to gain valuable teaching experience, and volunteer hours.
The Solution:
TutorMatch utilizes a unique approach to give each of our students, and tutors a more personalized and valuable academic experience. Using enhanced matching techniques through academic and behavioral analysis, TutorMatch will give the best support needed to each student. A peer tutoring model allows for a more collaborative and engaging learning environment. TutorMatch is free to use and tracks student performance over time, through the academic and behavioral ratings mentioned above, each tutor receives ratings from their students and each student is rated by the tutor. It uses those ratings, the requested subject by the student, the tutors preferred subjects, and conditional filters to develop a list of potential matches. Then the student will decide from a list of different tutor profiles, which will include key details regarding each tutor, including relevant experience. TutorMatch also has a custom scheduling interface designed to be adaptable to the timings and needs of various school systems. Currently, the scheduling interface follows the Monday through Friday blocks A-H system that The Academy of Aerospace and Engineering (AAE) uses. To ensure the safety of students using TutorMatch, every tutor needs to be manually verified by a trusted member of the program, or school administrator before they can be matched with a student. TutorMatch will fulfill its purpose by matching students with tutors through consideration of student academics and behavior and the requested subjects of both the tutor and student, while ensuring student safety through a tutor verification process.

## Conclusion:

TutorMatch addresses a critical gap in academic support in our school community by providing an accessible, reliable, and data-driven platform for teacher and peer-to-peer tutoring. Driven by the constraints we have identified in our local education system and the experiences we have within it, the platform is shaped accordingly to bridge the gap between students seeking help and knowledgeable peer tutors. Utilizing our skills acquired through the Advanced Placement Computer Science A course and other programming challenges, we successfully designed a Java-based implementation containing a sophisticated matching algorithm, factoring in academic and behavioral performance, to ensure student-tutor pairings are highly personalized and effective in improving the learning outcomes. 

Furthermore, by embedding our own manual verification process at our academic institution, the application prioritizes the safety, security, and learning of our student users. With TutorMatch, we not only build a platform for facilitating academic help; in the long-term, we envision TutorMatch fostering an engaging, collaborative learning environment embedded deep within the school’s own academic support systems. Our tool simultaneously provides struggling students with the resources they need to succeed, and empowers peer tutors with valuable experience and volunteering opportunities. By seamlessly integrating academic assistance within the daily lives of the student body’s school schedule, TutorMatch stands as a powerful solution for making education more equitable, effective, and community-driven here in our school, and in the future, worldwide.
